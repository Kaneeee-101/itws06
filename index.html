History

Three needs, one place

Pages & Pour was created from a problem students and readers share: finding one place to buy books, enjoy coffee, and study. That meant moving between three spots. A bookstore for school materials, a café for food and drinks, and a library or study space for work. Libraries are quiet, but you can't eat or talk. Coffee shops are fine for a drink, but not for hours of studying. Bookstores don't let you sit at all.

Pages & Pour brings those needs into one location. Customers can study, read, work, socialize, and enjoy their drinks and snacks without traveling somewhere else for each one.

A place to stay

Welcome to Pages & Pour

Looking for a cozy place where you can enjoy coffee, discover new books, study, or simply relax? Welcome to Pages & Pour!

Our bookstore and coffee shop brings together the simple things we love — good coffee, great books, and a comfortable study space. Whether you're a student looking for a quiet place to finish your work, a book lover searching for your next read, or someone who just wants to chill with a cup of coffee, Pages & Pour is a place for you.

Mission

To provide students, readers, and young professionals with a comfortable and convenient place to read, study, work, and relax while enjoying a nice coffee and having convenient access to books and learning materials without having to go somewhere else for each one.

Vision

To become the go-to neighborhood hub for students and readers, a place beyond home and school. Known for its books, brews, and its productive and welcoming atmosphere.
