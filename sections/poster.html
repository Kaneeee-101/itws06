CT#3 · Promotional event poster

Platter promo.

Thought process

  • Lead with the book — the repeating “BOOKS” cover is the hero of the promo
  • Pair a bookstore purchase with a light pour: iced drink + croissant at 20% off for a week
  • Hang the Pages & Pour visit sign so place and brand stay clear

Color scheme / palette

Warm gold wash, coffee-brown tray, cream paper on the book, deep ink for type.

Typography

Script display for “Platter promo.” All-caps serif for the offer line. Bold stacked sans on the book cover. Clean sans on the hanging visit sign.
